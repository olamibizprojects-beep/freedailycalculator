Cookie Policy

Last updated: May 14, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how FinanceTools Pro uses cookies and similar technologies when you visit our Website.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the site remember your preferences and improve your browsing experience.

How We Use Cookies

1. Essential Cookies

These cookies are necessary for the Website to function properly. They enable core features like dark mode preferences.

2. Analytics Cookies

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors interact with our Website. These cookies collect information about:

  • Pages you visit
  • Time spent on each page
  • Your general location (country/city level)
  • Device and browser information

This data helps us improve our calculators and content.

3. Advertising Cookies

We use Google AdSense to display relevant advertisements. These cookies:

  • Personalize ads based on your interests
  • Limit how often you see an ad
  • Measure ad effectiveness

Third-Party Cookies

The following third parties may set cookies when you use our Website:

  • Google Analytics: Traffic analysis
  • Google AdSense: Advertisement serving

Managing Cookies

You can control and manage cookies through your browser settings:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and Security → Cookies
  • Firefox: Options → Privacy & Security → Cookies
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Cookies
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions

You can also opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ad Settings.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy as technologies or regulations change. Updates will be posted on this page.
